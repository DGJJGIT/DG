Faire's business model has a detail that changes how fulfillment matters here versus traditional wholesale: Faire pays brands upfront and extends retailers Net 60 payment terms itself, absorbing the payment risk rather than making the brand wait 60 days to get paid. That means fulfillment speed isn't just about your own cash flow — it's part of what Faire is underwriting when it fronts the payment, and slow or inaccurate fulfillment reflects on the relationship regardless of when the retailer's own payment clears.

## The Free-Returns Window on Opening Orders Raises the Stakes on Accuracy

Faire offers new retailers a risk-free return window on their first order from a brand, designed to lower the barrier for a retailer trying a new supplier. That's genuinely useful for growing your retailer base, but it also means a picking error or damaged-in-transit item on a first order is more likely to actually come back — a retailer with nothing to lose returns an unsatisfactory opening order far more readily than one who's already committed to reordering. DeliveryGroup's barcode-verified pick-and-pack process is aimed at exactly this: getting the accuracy right on opening orders specifically, since that's when a mistake is cheapest for the retailer to walk away over.

## Case-Pack and Opening-Order Minimums Change the Shipment Profile

Faire lets brands set case-pack quantities, opening order minimums, and reorder minimums per product — meaning a single Faire order commonly ships as multiple units of the same SKU in case-pack configurations rather than the single-unit picks typical of a DTC order. DeliveryGroup fulfills these as genuine wholesale shipments: pallet or case-pack shipping with appropriate freight arrangements for larger orders, rather than treating every Faire order like an individual parcel pick.

## One Inventory Pool for Faire Wholesale and DTC

Brands selling on Faire almost always run a DTC channel alongside it — their own Shopify or BigCommerce storefront, sometimes Amazon or other marketplaces too. Keeping wholesale and DTC inventory artificially separate means holding more total stock than you actually need. DeliveryGroup fulfills both from one inventory pool at the Florence, KY facility: DTC orders ship parcel with branded packaging and fast ground delivery, Faire wholesale orders ship on pallets or in case-pack configurations with retailer-appropriate (non-retail-priced) packing slips, and both draw down the same underlying stock count.

## Faire's API for Order Sync

Faire provides a public API that DeliveryGroup connects through for order sync and tracking pushback, so Faire wholesale orders route into the same fulfillment queue as your other channels automatically rather than requiring manual entry from the Faire brand dashboard.
