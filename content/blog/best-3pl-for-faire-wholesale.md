## How Wholesale and DTC Fulfillment Are Different

If you have only done DTC fulfillment, wholesale orders will surprise you. The differences go beyond just order size. The entire process is different.

DTC orders are simple. A customer buys one or two items. You pick them from the shelf, pack them in a branded box, and ship via UPS or USPS. The package weighs a few pounds. It arrives at a house in 3 to 5 days.

Wholesale orders are more complex. A retailer orders 200 units across 10 SKUs. Those units need to be packed in specific case quantities. The cases need to go on a pallet. The pallet needs to be wrapped and labeled. The shipment goes via LTL freight to a receiving dock at a retail store or distribution center.

The labeling requirements are different. Retailers often have specific barcode and label requirements. Some need GS1 labels. Some need custom packing slips. Some require advance ship notices (ASNs) before the shipment arrives.

The timelines are different too. Wholesale orders often have specific ship-by dates tied to purchase orders. Missing those dates can result in chargebacks or lost accounts.

If you try to run both workflows in the same space without a system, mistakes happen. Wholesale inventory gets mixed with DTC inventory. Orders get shipped with the wrong packaging. Deadlines get missed. It becomes chaos.

## Bulk Packing for Wholesale Orders

Wholesale fulfillment requires a different kind of packing. You are not putting items in poly mailers and slapping on a shipping label. You are building cases, packing cartons, and preparing shipments that meet retailer requirements.

DeliveryGroup has dedicated wholesale packing workflows. Here is what we handle for Faire sellers.

**Inner pack assembly.** We pack products into inner cartons in the quantities your retailers expect. If a retailer orders in case packs of 12, we build those cases to spec.

**Outer carton packing.** Inner packs go into outer shipping cartons. We use the right box size for each order to minimize dimensional weight charges and protect the product in transit.

**Custom labeling.** We apply retailer-specific labels to each carton. This includes UPC barcodes, case labels, and any other markings the retailer requires.

**Packing slips and documentation.** Wholesale orders often require detailed packing slips that list contents by SKU and quantity. We generate these automatically based on the order data.

**Quality inspection.** Before sealing cartons, we verify the contents match the order. This prevents short-ships and incorrect quantities that lead to chargebacks.

Our warehouse team is trained in wholesale packing standards. They know how to build a clean, tight case pack. They know how labels need to be positioned. They treat every wholesale order like a professional operation because that is what your retail partners expect.

## Palletizing and Freight Shipping

Large wholesale orders ship by freight, not by parcel. That means pallets. And pallets need to be built right or they cause problems at the receiving dock.

DeliveryGroup handles palletizing for all wholesale orders that require it. We build pallets to industry standards. Cartons are stacked correctly for stability. Stretch wrap holds everything in place. Labels go on all four sides so the receiving team can identify the shipment from any angle.

We work with LTL (less-than-truckload) carriers for most wholesale shipments. Our system generates bills of lading, tracks shipments, and provides delivery confirmation. For larger orders, we arrange full truckload shipping.

For Faire sellers, freight shipping can be intimidating if you have never done it before. We handle the logistics end to end. You do not need to call freight brokers or figure out freight classes. Just tell us where the order is going and we take care of the rest.

We also optimize your freight costs. Our volume gives us access to negotiated freight rates. We compare options across carriers to find the best combination of price and transit time for each shipment.

DeliveryGroup manages the B2B shipping process for Faire sellers. Here is what that includes.

Let us walk through what happens when a Faire order comes in.

Tracking information flows back to Faire and to your notification system. The retailer gets a shipment notification. You get confirmation that the order shipped.
