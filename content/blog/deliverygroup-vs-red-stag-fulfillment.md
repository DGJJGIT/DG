Red Stag Fulfillment and DeliveryGroup are both 3PLs that operate from strategic central US locations. Red Stag is based in Knoxville, Tennessee. DeliveryGroup is based in Florence, Kentucky. Both use their geographic advantage to offer fast ground shipping to most of the country.

But there are meaningful differences between the two. This article breaks down where each one shines so you can pick the right partner for your brand.

## Location and Shipping Reach

Both companies benefit from central US locations that put them within 2 to 3 day ground shipping of the majority of the US population.

Red Stag operates from over 1.2 million square feet of warehouse space in Knoxville. They claim to reach 96 percent of the US in 2 days by ground.

DeliveryGroup's 75,000 square foot facility in Northern Kentucky sits 5 miles from DHL's CVG Super Hub, one of only three in the world. This direct-inject relationship with DHL means packages enter the global shipping network at the earliest possible point. DeliveryGroup reaches over 80 percent of the US in 2 to 3 days by ground.

The key difference is the DHL partnership. DeliveryGroup does not just ship from a good location. They inject directly into DHL's hub, which gives them both speed and cost advantages that a standard carrier pickup cannot match.

## Product Specialization

Red Stag has built its reputation on **big, heavy, and high-value products**. If you sell furniture, fitness equipment, electronics, or other oversized items, Red Stag has specialized handling, packaging, and shipping for that category.

DeliveryGroup serves a **broader range of product types** including standard e-commerce, apparel, supplements, subscription boxes, and fragile items. They have built specific capabilities for supplement fulfillment with lot tracking, FEFO rotation, and FDA-compliant storage.

If your primary products are oversized and heavy, Red Stag is purpose-built for that. If you sell across multiple product categories or need specialized supplement handling, DeliveryGroup is more versatile.

## FBA Prep

This is a significant differentiator. **DeliveryGroup offers full Amazon FBA prep services** from the same facility. FNSKU labeling, poly bagging, bundling, inspection, and shipping plan creation. Their proximity to Amazon's CVG Air Hub means same-day inbound delivery.

**Red Stag does not offer FBA prep.** If you sell on Amazon through FBA, you would need a separate prep center. With DeliveryGroup, FBA prep and merchant fulfilled orders run from the same inventory pool.

## Performance Guarantees

Red Stag is known for their **financial-backed performance guarantees**. They literally pay you if they miss their accuracy, shrinkage, or shipping SLAs. This is a strong trust signal and a genuine differentiator in the 3PL space.

DeliveryGroup maintains a **99.4 percent on-time delivery rate** and offers service-level guarantees for enterprise clients. While they do not publicly advertise a pay-if-we-fail model like Red Stag, their performance metrics are consistently strong and they provide real-time performance dashboards.

## Technology

Both offer real-time order tracking, inventory management, and analytics dashboards.

Red Stag provides a client portal with live 90-day trailing performance data displayed publicly on their website. This transparency is impressive.

DeliveryGroup integrates with over 200 platforms and offers a REST API for custom integrations. Their dashboard includes GPS tracking, exception alerts, and automated branded customer notifications via SMS and email.

## Pricing

Red Stag tends to position as a premium provider, especially for the big and heavy category. Their per-order fees reflect the specialized handling these products require.

DeliveryGroup's pricing starts at $3.00 per order for pick and pack, with $25 per pallet per month for storage. Their DHL partnership passes discounted shipping rates directly to clients. For most standard e-commerce products, DeliveryGroup's total cost per shipment is competitive.

## Who Is Each Best For?

**Red Stag is a good fit if:**
- You sell big, heavy, or high-value products
- Financial-backed performance guarantees are important to you
- You do not need FBA prep
- You want a 3PL with deep expertise in oversized fulfillment

**DeliveryGroup is a good fit if:**
- You need FBA prep and 3PL fulfillment together
- You sell supplements and need lot tracking and FEFO
- You want the lowest shipping rates through DHL's direct partnership
- You sell across multiple product categories
- You want a dedicated account manager from day one

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Which 3PL is cheaper, Red Stag or DeliveryGroup?**
For standard-sized products, DeliveryGroup is typically more cost-effective. For oversized and heavy products, Red Stag's specialized handling may be worth the premium.

**Does Red Stag offer FBA prep?**
No. Red Stag focuses on DTC and B2B fulfillment. DeliveryGroup offers full FBA prep services.

**Can DeliveryGroup handle heavy or oversized items?**
Yes. DeliveryGroup handles oversized products, though it is not their primary specialization the way it is for Red Stag.

**Which one has better shipping rates?**
DeliveryGroup's direct DHL partnership generally provides lower shipping rates for ground shipping nationwide. Red Stag works with multiple carriers and negotiates competitive rates, particularly for heavy parcels.

**How do their accuracy rates compare?**
Red Stag publishes live 90-day accuracy data (typically 99.99 percent). DeliveryGroup maintains a 99.4 percent on-time delivery rate across all modalities.

**Can I switch from Red Stag to DeliveryGroup?**
Yes. DeliveryGroup's implementation team manages the full transition. Most migrations complete in 2 to 4 weeks.
