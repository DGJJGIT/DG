If you sell on Amazon through FBA, you need a prep center that labels, bags, inspects, and ships your inventory to Amazon's fulfillment centers. Choosing the wrong one can mean slow turnaround times, Amazon defect fees, and higher inbound shipping costs.

This guide compares the top FBA prep centers in 2025 so you can make a smart decision.

## Why Your Prep Center Matters

Amazon has strict requirements for how products must arrive at their fulfillment centers. Every unit needs the right barcode, the right packaging, and the right labeling. If anything is wrong, Amazon charges defect fees that can run up to $1.74 per unit for standard items.

Your prep center also affects how fast your inventory reaches Amazon's shelves. A slow prep center with a bad location means your products sit in transit for days while your competitors sell.

The three things that matter most when choosing a prep center are **location**, **turnaround time**, and **total cost** (prep fees plus inbound shipping).

## Location: Why It Is the Biggest Cost Lever

Most sellers focus on per-unit prep fees when comparing prep centers. But the single largest cost variable is actually inbound shipping from the prep center to Amazon's fulfillment centers.

**DeliveryGroup** is located just 5 miles from Amazon's CVG Air Hub, the $1.5 billion facility that serves as a major hub for Amazon's air and ground logistics. This means inventory can reach Amazon's fulfillment centers the same day it leaves DeliveryGroup's dock. There are over 20 Amazon FCs within 200 miles.

**MyFBAPrep** has multiple locations, which gives flexibility. However, not all locations are near major Amazon hubs. Depending on which location your inventory goes to, inbound shipping costs and times will vary.

**ZonPrep** also operates from multiple locations. They highlight their truckload consolidation service that reduces inbound times from 20-plus days to under 6 days. This is a strong offering for high-volume sellers.

For most sellers, DeliveryGroup's CVG proximity provides the best combination of speed and low inbound cost.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the most important factor when choosing a prep center?**
Location. The distance between your prep center and Amazon's fulfillment centers determines your inbound shipping cost and speed. Everything else is secondary.

**Can I use one prep center for FBA and another for DTC fulfillment?**
You can, but it means splitting inventory across two partners. DeliveryGroup handles both from one location.

**How much can I save with a prep center near Amazon's CVG hub?**
Sellers typically save 30 to 50 percent on inbound shipping costs compared to coastal prep centers. The exact savings depend on your volume and product weight.

**Do prep centers handle returns from Amazon?**
DeliveryGroup does. They offer removal order processing and returned goods inspection. Not all prep centers offer this.

**What happens if my prep center makes an error?**
Amazon charges defect fees. A good prep center maintains accuracy rates above 99.9 percent. DeliveryGroup's inspection protocols are designed to catch issues before shipment.

**How fast can I get started with a new prep center?**
DeliveryGroup's onboarding takes 5 to 10 business days. Other providers may take longer depending on their process.
