## Why Walmart's Seller Standards Matter

Walmart does not mess around with seller performance. They track several key metrics for every seller on the platform. If you fall below their thresholds, you risk losing your listing visibility or getting suspended entirely.

Here are the main metrics Walmart watches closely:

- **On-time shipping rate.** Walmart expects you to ship orders within the window you promise. Late shipments hurt your score fast.
- **Valid tracking rate.** Every order needs a working tracking number uploaded to Walmart's system. No exceptions.
- **Cancellation rate.** If you cancel too many orders because you are out of stock, Walmart will flag your account.
- **Return and refund rate.** High return rates signal quality or listing accuracy problems.
- **Delivery defect rate.** This covers late deliveries, missing packages, and anything else that goes wrong after the order ships.

Walmart reviews these metrics regularly. Sellers who consistently miss targets get warnings first. Then they get suspended. A suspension on Walmart is hard to recover from. You lose your buy box placement, your reviews disappear from search, and rebuilding trust takes months.

The bottom line is simple. You need a fulfillment partner that treats Walmart's standards as seriously as you do.

## The Risk of Account Suspension

Let us be blunt about what happens when your Walmart seller account gets suspended. It is not just a temporary inconvenience. It can seriously damage your business.

When Walmart suspends your account, all of your listings go offline immediately. Customers can no longer find or purchase your products. Any open orders may need to be cancelled, which further damages your metrics. Your seller history and review accumulation stop dead.

Getting reinstated requires submitting a detailed corrective action plan to Walmart. You have to explain what went wrong, what caused the problem, and exactly what steps you are taking to fix it. Walmart reviews these plans carefully and does not rush the process. Some sellers wait weeks to get reinstated. Others never get their accounts back.

The most common reasons for suspension are repeated late shipments, high cancellation rates, and a pattern of delivery defects. All of these problems trace back to fulfillment. If your 3PL is unreliable, your Walmart account is at risk every single day.

## Walmart Fulfillment Services vs. Using a 3PL

Walmart offers its own fulfillment program called Walmart Fulfillment Services, or WFS. It works similar to Amazon's FBA program. You send your inventory to Walmart's warehouses, and they handle storage, packing, and shipping.

WFS has some advantages. Your products get the "Fulfilled by Walmart" badge, which can boost conversions. You also get access to Walmart's two-day shipping promise.

But WFS is not perfect for every seller. Here are some reasons a 3PL like DeliveryGroup might be a better fit:

**More control over your brand.** With WFS, Walmart packs your products in plain Walmart boxes. With DeliveryGroup, you can use custom packaging, branded inserts, and thank-you cards. Your brand stays front and center.

**Multi-channel fulfillment.** If you sell on Walmart, Amazon, Shopify, and eBay, WFS only handles your Walmart orders. DeliveryGroup fulfills orders from all your channels out of the same inventory pool. That means less complexity and fewer stockouts.

**Flexible storage terms.** WFS charges storage fees that can add up fast, especially for slow-moving products. DeliveryGroup offers transparent pricing with flexible storage options that work for businesses of all sizes.

**Faster problem resolution.** When something goes wrong with WFS, you are dealing with Walmart's support system. With DeliveryGroup, you have a dedicated account manager who picks up the phone. Problems get solved in hours, not days.

**No strict product restrictions.** WFS has specific requirements about product types, packaging, and labeling. Some products do not qualify. DeliveryGroup works with a wider range of products and can accommodate special handling needs.
