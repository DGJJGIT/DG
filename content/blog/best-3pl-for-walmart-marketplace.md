Walmart Marketplace tracks seller performance against specific named metrics — Valid Tracking Rate (VTR) and on-time shipping chief among them — that feed directly into account standing and search visibility. Fall below Walmart's thresholds and the consequence isn't a warning email, it's suppressed listings and, in serious cases, account review.

## Valid Tracking Rate Is Its Own Metric, Separate From Shipping Speed

Walmart requires a valid, carrier-scannable tracking number uploaded promptly for every order — and tracks the percentage of orders meeting that standard as Valid Tracking Rate, independent of whether the shipment itself arrived on time. A package that ships promptly but gets an invalid or late-uploaded tracking number still hurts VTR the same way a genuinely late shipment does. DeliveryGroup pushes tracking to Walmart automatically the moment a label generates, tying tracking upload timing to the actual carrier handoff rather than a delayed batch process.

## Two Fulfillment Paths: Walmart Fulfillment Services vs. Seller Fulfilled

Walmart Marketplace sellers choose between Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS), where Walmart stores and ships inventory similarly to Amazon FBA, and Seller Fulfilled, where the seller (or their 3PL) handles storage and shipping directly. DeliveryGroup fulfills the Seller Fulfilled path, which gives you control over packaging, branding, and inventory allocation that WFS's more standardized model doesn't offer — while still needing to hit the same on-time-shipping and tracking standards Walmart holds every seller to, regardless of fulfillment method.

## The 2-Day Delivery Badge Requires Consistency, Not Just Capability

Walmart's 2-Day (and in some cases 1-Day) delivery badges are awarded to listings that can reliably meet those delivery windows, and badge eligibility is based on consistent historical performance, not a one-time capability check. A single missed window doesn't necessarily strip the badge, but a pattern of inconsistency does — which makes DeliveryGroup's Florence, Kentucky location genuinely relevant here: reaching roughly 80% of US addresses in 2–3 days via standard ground gives real margin to hit 2-Day delivery commitments consistently, rather than requiring expedited shipping on every order just to stay within Walmart's window.

## Item Setup Quality Affects Search Independent of Fulfillment

Walmart's search ranking also weighs listing content quality (complete, accurate item specifics, imagery) alongside seller performance metrics — a detail worth mentioning because sellers sometimes attribute a search-visibility drop entirely to fulfillment performance when listing quality is part of the equation too. Fulfillment performance and listing quality are separate levers; DeliveryGroup's role is making sure the fulfillment side of that equation stays solid so it's never the thing holding a well-built listing back.
