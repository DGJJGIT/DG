## What Makes BigCommerce Different

BigCommerce is a hosted e-commerce platform, which means it runs in the cloud. You do not need to install software or manage servers. But unlike some other hosted platforms, BigCommerce is designed with enterprise-level features built in from the start.

Here are some things that make BigCommerce stand out:

- **No transaction fees:** Unlike some competitors, BigCommerce does not charge extra fees on top of your payment processor's charges. Every sale you make costs less.
- **Multi-channel selling:** BigCommerce connects natively to Amazon, eBay, Walmart, Facebook, Instagram, Google Shopping, and more. You can manage all your listings from one dashboard.
- **Headless commerce support:** For brands that want a custom front-end experience, BigCommerce supports headless architecture through APIs. You can build any front end you want while keeping BigCommerce as your back end.
- **B2B features:** BigCommerce has built-in tools for wholesale and business-to-business selling, including customer groups, price lists, and quote management.
- **High scalability:** BigCommerce handles high traffic volumes and large product catalogs without performance issues. No worrying about your site crashing during a sale.

These features make BigCommerce popular with growing brands and established businesses that sell across multiple channels. But all of those channels generate orders that need to be fulfilled. That is where the right 3PL makes all the difference.

## Why BigCommerce Sellers Need a Multi-Channel 3PL

If you only sold on your BigCommerce website, fulfillment would be relatively simple. But most BigCommerce sellers take advantage of the platform's multi-channel capabilities. They sell on their own site, Amazon, eBay, Walmart, and social media all at the same time.

Multi-channel selling creates fulfillment challenges that a single-channel seller never faces:

- **Inventory must be accurate across all channels.** If you sell the last unit of a product on Amazon, your BigCommerce store needs to reflect that immediately. Otherwise, you oversell and disappoint a customer. That leads to refunds, bad reviews, and potential penalties.
- **Orders come from different places.** You need a system that pulls orders from every channel and routes them to the warehouse for fulfillment. Manual management becomes impossible as you add channels.
- **Shipping requirements vary.** Amazon has specific shipping and labeling requirements. Walmart has its own rules. Your own website might have custom packaging needs. The 3PL needs to handle all of it without mixing things up.
- **Returns come from different channels.** Each channel may have different return policies and processes. The 3PL needs to manage returns regardless of where the original order came from.

A 3PL that only works with one platform is not going to cut it. You need a partner that understands multi-channel fulfillment and can keep everything in sync. That is exactly what DeliveryGroup provides.

## Kitting and Product Bundling

Kitting is the process of combining multiple items into a single package. If you sell a gift set that includes a candle, a soap bar, and a card, those three items need to be assembled into one box before shipping. That is kitting.

BigCommerce sellers use kitting for many different purposes:

- **Product bundles:** Selling multiple related items together at a discount. For example, a skincare set with cleanser, toner, and moisturizer.
- **Subscription boxes:** Monthly or quarterly curated packages with changing contents.
- **Gift sets:** Holiday or occasion-specific product combinations that are ready to give.
- **Starter kits:** Collections of items that help new customers get started with a product line.
- **Promotional packs:** Limited-time bundles tied to marketing campaigns or seasonal events.
- **Variety packs:** Assortments that let customers try multiple flavors, scents, or styles.

DeliveryGroup handles kitting for BigCommerce sellers. You provide the instructions for what goes in each kit, and the warehouse team assembles them. Kits can be pre-built and stored as ready-to-ship units, or they can be assembled on demand as orders come in.

The kitting process also includes any special packaging requirements. If a gift set needs tissue paper, a ribbon, and a branded card, the team includes all of that. The result is a polished product that looks great when the customer opens it.

Pre-building kits ahead of busy seasons like the holidays is a smart strategy. DeliveryGroup can assemble hundreds or thousands of kits in advance so they are ready to ship the moment orders come in. That keeps fulfillment fast even during your busiest periods.

## Lot Tracking and Expiration Management

Some BigCommerce sellers deal with products that have lot numbers, batch codes, or expiration dates. This is common in categories like food, supplements, beauty products, and health goods.

Lot tracking means knowing exactly which batch of a product was sent to which customer. This is important for several reasons:

- **Recalls:** If a specific batch has an issue, you need to know who received it so you can notify those customers quickly.
- **Expiration management:** Products with expiration dates need to be shipped on a first-in, first-out basis. The oldest stock should go out first to prevent items from expiring on the shelf.
- **Regulatory compliance:** Some industries require lot tracking for compliance with FDA or other regulatory bodies. Having accurate records is not optional in these categories.
- **Quality control:** Tracking lots helps you identify patterns if customers report issues with a specific batch. You can trace the problem back to a specific production run.

DeliveryGroup supports lot tracking and expiration date management for BigCommerce sellers. The warehouse team records lot information when inventory arrives and uses first-in, first-out rotation to ensure products ship before they expire. If a product is approaching its expiration date, the team can flag it so you can decide whether to discount it, donate it, or dispose of it.

This is a feature that not every 3PL offers. If you sell products with lot numbers or expiration dates, make sure your fulfillment partner can handle it. DeliveryGroup can.
