*This comparison is published by DeliveryGroup. Competitor details reflect their public materials at the time of writing; confirm current services and pricing with each provider.*

If you are shopping for a 3PL, ShipMonk and DeliveryGroup are both names worth considering. They serve similar markets but take different approaches to technology, pricing, and service. This comparison will help you figure out which one makes more sense for your brand.

## Company Overview

**ShipMonk** is a technology-focused 3PL headquartered in Florida with multiple fulfillment centers across the US. They are known for their software platform, AI-powered carrier optimization, and strong DTC subscription box capabilities.

**DeliveryGroup** operates from a 75,000 square foot facility in Northern Kentucky, 5 miles from Amazon's CVG Air Hub and DHL's CVG Super Hub. They combine 3PL fulfillment with Amazon FBA prep under one roof, with a direct DHL partnership for discounted shipping rates.

## Technology Platforms

ShipMonk invests heavily in technology. Their platform includes an AI-powered "Virtual Carrier Network" that selects the best carrier for each shipment based on speed and cost. They also have strong inventory management and analytics tools.

DeliveryGroup's platform provides real-time shipment visibility with GPS tracking, delivery status updates, exception alerts, and performance reporting. They integrate with over 200 e-commerce platforms, marketplaces, and ERPs. For enterprise clients, they offer custom API and EDI integrations.

Both platforms are solid. ShipMonk leans more into AI-driven carrier selection, while DeliveryGroup's DHL direct-inject partnership provides consistently low rates without needing to shop across carriers.

## Shipping Costs

This is where the models diverge significantly.

ShipMonk works with multiple carriers and uses their AI to pick the cheapest or fastest option for each order. This can result in good rates, but it also means your shipping costs vary from order to order.

DeliveryGroup's primary shipping advantage is their direct DHL partnership. Their facility is 5 miles from DHL's CVG Super Hub, one of only three in the world. By injecting packages directly into DHL's global network, they achieve transit times and rates that are hard to match. This consistency makes it easier to predict your shipping costs month to month.

## FBA Prep

**DeliveryGroup offers full FBA prep services** including FNSKU labeling, poly bagging, bundling, shrink wrapping, inspection, and shipping plan creation. Their proximity to Amazon's CVG Air Hub keeps the trip to Amazon short.

**ShipMonk also offers FBA prep services.** The difference is the model: ShipMonk runs a technology-led, multi-location network, while DeliveryGroup runs FBA prep and DTC fulfillment from the same warehouse and the same inventory.

## Supplement and Nutraceutical Fulfillment

Both companies handle supplement fulfillment, but DeliveryGroup has built specific capabilities for this vertical. They offer lot-level tracking, FEFO inventory rotation, expiration date management, and FDA-compliant storage. Subscription order automation handles recurring supplement orders across DTC, Amazon, and wholesale channels.

ShipMonk can handle supplements as well, but DeliveryGroup positions this as a core specialty rather than an add-on.

## Onboarding and Service

ShipMonk's onboarding is largely self-service through their software platform. You set up your account, configure your integrations, and ship inventory to their warehouse. Support is available through their system.

DeliveryGroup's team handles your tech setup, SKU mapping, platform integration, and test orders. Once live, you have a dedicated point of contact who handles day-to-day account communication. Most clients go live in 5 to 10 business days.

## Pricing Transparency

ShipMonk's pricing includes pick and pack fees, storage fees, and shipping. They sometimes offer promotional pricing for new clients. Fees can add up with special handling, custom packaging, and other add-ons.

DeliveryGroup provides transparent pricing, with every fee published upfront. Pick and pack starts at $3.00 for the first item. Storage is $25 per pallet per month.

## Who Is Each Best For?

**ShipMonk is a good fit if:**
- You want AI-driven carrier optimization across multiple carriers
- You are focused primarily on DTC subscription box fulfillment
- You prefer a self-service, software-first onboarding experience
- You prefer a software-first, multi-location 3PL

**DeliveryGroup is a good fit if:**
- You need FBA prep and 3PL fulfillment under one roof
- You want consistently low shipping rates through DHL's direct partnership
- You sell supplements and need lot tracking, FEFO, and FDA compliance
- You value dedicated account management over self-service
- You want to go live in under 10 business days with hands-on support

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is ShipMonk or DeliveryGroup cheaper?**
It depends on your product weight, shipping zones, and volume. DeliveryGroup's DHL rates are often lower for ground shipping nationwide. ShipMonk's carrier optimization can find deals on specific lanes. Request quotes from both to compare.

**Can I do FBA prep with ShipMonk?**
Yes, ShipMonk offers FBA prep services. DeliveryGroup's difference is that FBA prep and DTC fulfillment run from one Northern Kentucky building and one inventory pool.

**Which is better for subscription boxes?**
Both handle subscription fulfillment. ShipMonk has a strong reputation in this space. DeliveryGroup offers kitting, custom packaging, and marketing inserts for subscription orders as well.

**How long does onboarding take?**
ShipMonk's self-service setup can take 1 to 3 weeks depending on complexity. DeliveryGroup's hands-on onboarding typically goes live in 5 to 10 business days.

**Do both handle international shipping?**
Yes. DeliveryGroup ships to over 100 countries through their DHL partnership. ShipMonk also supports international shipping through their carrier network.

**Can I switch from ShipMonk to DeliveryGroup?**
Yes. DeliveryGroup's team manages the transition with you. Most migrations take 2 to 4 weeks.
