## Why Mercari Sellers Need a 3PL

Most Mercari sellers start by doing everything themselves. They source products. They photograph items. They write listings. They store inventory in their home. They pack orders. They drive to the post office.

This works when you sell 5 or 10 items a week. But when you sell 50, 100, or 500 items a week, doing it all yourself is not possible. Something has to give.

A 3PL handles the physical side of your business. You send your inventory to the warehouse. When an order comes in, the 3PL picks the item, packs it, and ships it. You never have to touch a box or stand in line at the post office again.

This frees up your time for the parts of the business that actually make money. You can spend more time sourcing great products. You can improve your listings. You can expand to new platforms. The fulfillment happens in the background while you focus on growth.

## Barcoded Inventory for Accuracy

One of the biggest challenges for resellers is keeping track of inventory. When you have hundreds or thousands of unique items, it is easy to lose track. Where did you put that vintage jacket? Which bin has the electronics?

DeliveryGroup barcodes every item that comes into our warehouse. Each product gets a unique barcode and a spot in our system. You can see exactly what you have in stock at any time.

This level of organization prevents mistakes. When a Mercari order comes in, our team scans the barcode to find the right item. They pick it off the shelf and pack it up. No guessing. No searching through piles of inventory.

For resellers with large catalogs of one-of-a-kind items, this system is critical. Every vintage shirt, used book, or refurbished gadget has its own place and its own tracking code.
