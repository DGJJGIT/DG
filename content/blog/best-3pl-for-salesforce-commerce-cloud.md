## 99.5% Order Accuracy or Better

Enterprise brands cannot afford fulfillment mistakes. Every wrong item, missing item, or incorrect quantity damages your reputation. It creates customer service tickets. It costs money in reshipping and returns. It erodes the trust you have built with your customers.

DeliveryGroup maintains a 99.5% or higher order accuracy rate. Here is how we do it.

Every product in our warehouse gets a unique barcode location. When a picker walks to a bin, they scan both the location barcode and the product barcode. If they do not match, the system stops them. The wrong item cannot make it into the order.

At the packing station, every item gets scanned again. The system verifies that the right products are in the box before the packer can print a shipping label. This double verification catches the rare errors that make it past the picking stage.

For brands with strict accuracy requirements, we offer additional quality checkpoints. We can weigh packages to verify contents. We can photograph orders before sealing them. We can implement lot tracking and serial number verification.

The result is a fulfillment process that your customers can rely on. They get what they ordered, in the right quantity, in the right condition, every time.

Here are some specific ways we help Salesforce Commerce Cloud brands save on shipping.

**Negotiated rates.** Our shipping volume across all clients gives us negotiating power with carriers. Our clients benefit from rates they could not get on their own.
