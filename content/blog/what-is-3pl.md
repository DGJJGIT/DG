**A 3PL (third-party logistics provider) is a company that handles your storage, order fulfillment, and shipping for you** — so you don't run a warehouse or manage carriers yourself. If you sell products and someone else stores your inventory, picks and packs your orders, and ships them to your customers, that "someone else" is a 3PL.

## 3PL meaning, in one sentence

Third-party logistics = outsourcing the physical side of getting products from you to your customer. The "third party" is the logistics partner sitting between your business and your buyer.

## What does a 3PL actually do?

A full-service 3PL typically covers:

- **Receiving & warehousing** — taking in your inventory and storing it. See [3PL warehousing](/3pl-warehousing).
- **Pick & pack** — pulling the right items for each order and packing them. See [pick and pack services](/pick-and-pack-services).
- **Shipping** — choosing carriers and getting orders out the door, often with same-day cut-offs.
- **Inventory management** — real-time stock visibility through a [WMS](/3pl-wms).
- **Returns / reverse logistics** — processing what comes back. See [returns management](/returns-management).
- **Value-adds** — [kitting](/kitting-services), branded packaging, and specialty handling like [hazmat logistics](/hazmat-logistics).

## How does a 3PL work?

1. You send your inventory to the 3PL's warehouse.
2. Your store connects to the 3PL so orders flow in automatically.
3. The 3PL picks, packs, and ships each order.
4. You watch inventory and order status in real time — and scale up without adding a warehouse.

## How much does a 3PL cost?

3PL pricing is usually **per order plus storage**, sometimes with receiving and value-add fees. There is rarely a single sticker price because it depends on SKU count, order volume, and services used.

## When should you use a 3PL?

Consider a 3PL when packing orders is eating time you should spend growing, when you're expanding to new regions and want faster delivery, or when volume (or a viral spike) outgrows what you can ship by hand. See [3PL ecommerce fulfillment](/3pl-ecommerce-fulfillment) and [small business fulfillment](/small-business-fulfillment).

## Frequently asked questions

**What does 3PL stand for?** Third-party logistics.

**Is a 3PL the same as a warehouse?** No — a warehouse just stores goods; a 3PL stores *and* fulfills orders (pick, pack, ship, returns).

**What's the difference between 3PL and dropshipping?** With a 3PL you own the inventory and it's stored on your behalf; with dropshipping you don't hold inventory at all.
