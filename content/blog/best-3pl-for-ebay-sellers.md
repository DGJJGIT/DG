## Why Your eBay Feedback Score Is So Important

On eBay, your feedback score is public. Every buyer who purchases from you can leave a rating. Positive feedback builds your reputation. Negative feedback damages it. And unlike some other platforms, eBay makes your feedback history very visible to potential buyers.

But feedback is not just about reputation. eBay's search algorithm, called Best Match, uses your seller performance to decide where your listings appear in search results. Sellers with high feedback scores, fast shipping, and low defect rates get pushed to the top. Sellers with problems get buried.

Here is what eBay's Best Match algorithm considers:

- **Seller feedback rating.** Higher is better. Buyers trust sellers with thousands of positive reviews.
- **Shipping speed.** Listings with fast handling times and quick delivery rank higher.
- **Tracking uploads.** eBay wants to see tracking numbers uploaded promptly for every order.
- **Defect rate.** This includes late shipments, items not as described, and cases opened against you.
- **Return experience.** How smoothly you handle returns affects your overall seller rating.

If your fulfillment is sloppy, every one of these metrics suffers. Your listings drop in search. Your sales decline. It becomes a downward spiral that is hard to escape.

## What Top Rated Seller Status Gets You

eBay's Top Rated Seller program rewards the best sellers on the platform. To qualify, you need to meet strict performance standards over a rolling 12-month period. The requirements include a very low defect rate, a high on-time shipping rate, and a solid track record of resolved cases.

When you earn Top Rated Seller status, eBay gives you real benefits:

- **A badge on your listings.** Buyers see the Top Rated Seller seal and feel more confident purchasing from you.
- **Better search visibility.** eBay boosts Top Rated Sellers in Best Match results.
- **Lower final value fees.** Top Rated Sellers with same-day or one-day handling get a discount on eBay's selling fees.
- **More buyer trust.** The badge signals reliability. Shoppers are more likely to choose your listing over a competitor's.

Losing Top Rated Seller status means losing all of those advantages. Your listings drop in search. Your fees go up. Your conversion rate falls. It is a significant hit to your bottom line.

This is exactly why your fulfillment has to be rock solid. Every late shipment or missing tracking number puts your status at risk.
