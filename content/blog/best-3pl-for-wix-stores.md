## The DIY Shipping Wall

The DIY shipping wall is real. It hits when your order volume outgrows your ability to handle it alone. There are a few signs you have reached it.

You spend more time on shipping than on marketing. You miss shipping deadlines because you ran out of boxes. Your living space is full of inventory and supplies. You dread weekends because that is when you catch up on orders. Customers email about slow shipping.

If any of these sound familiar, you are at the wall. And here is the thing. You cannot break through it by working harder. There are only so many hours in a day. The answer is not more hustle. The answer is a system.

A 3PL is that system. You send your inventory to a warehouse. When orders come in, the warehouse ships them. You get your time and your home back. Your customers get their orders faster. Everyone wins.

## SKU Mapping Made Easy

SKU mapping sounds technical, but it is really simple. It means matching your product codes to the items in our warehouse. When your Wix store sends an order for "Blue Widget Large," our system knows exactly which item on which shelf to grab.

DeliveryGroup handles SKU mapping during onboarding. We work with your product catalog to make sure every item in your Wix store is mapped correctly to our warehouse inventory.

This is important for accuracy. When a customer orders a specific size, color, or variation, the right item ships every time. No mix-ups. No wrong items. No frustrated customers.

If you add new products later, we update the mapping. It is a quick process that keeps your fulfillment running smoothly as your catalog grows.
