# Best 3PL for Shopify Sellers: Why DeliveryGroup Is the Smart Choice in 2025

Running a Shopify store is exciting. You pick your products, build your brand, and watch orders come in. But at some point, packing boxes in your garage stops making sense. That is where a 3PL comes in.

## What Is a 3PL?

A 3PL stands for third-party logistics. It is a company that stores your products, picks and packs your orders, and ships them to your customers. Instead of doing all of that yourself, you hand it off to a team that does it every day.

Think of it like hiring a warehouse, a packing crew, and a shipping department all at once. You send your inventory to the 3PL. When a customer places an order on your Shopify store, the 3PL picks the items, packs them, and ships them out. You never have to touch a box.

A good 3PL also handles returns, tracks inventory, and gives you data about what is selling and what is sitting on the shelf. The goal is simple. You focus on growing your business. They handle getting products to your customers.

## Why Shopify Sellers Need a 3PL

Shopify makes it easy to sell online. But selling is only half the job. You also have to get the product to the customer. That part gets harder as you grow.

Here are some signs you need a 3PL:

- You are spending more time packing orders than growing your business.
- Your living room or garage is full of inventory.
- Shipping costs are eating into your profits.
- Customers are complaining about slow delivery.
- You want to offer two-day or three-day shipping but cannot do it on your own.
- You are getting ready for a big product launch or holiday season.
- You are missing orders or making packing mistakes because you are overwhelmed.

If any of these sound familiar, it is time to look at a 3PL. The sooner you make the switch, the sooner you can stop being a warehouse worker and start being a business owner again.

## Subscription Box Kitting

Subscription boxes are one of the fastest-growing segments in e-commerce. If you run a subscription box business on Shopify, you need a 3PL that understands kitting.

Kitting means putting together a specific set of items into one package. For a subscription box, that might mean combining five or six different products into a single box each month. Every month, the mix might change.

DeliveryGroup handles kitting for subscription box sellers. You tell the team what goes in each box, and they assemble them ahead of time. When the orders come in, the boxes are ready to ship. This keeps your subscription running smoothly without delays.

Here is how the kitting process works at DeliveryGroup:

1. You send the individual products to the warehouse.
2. You provide a kit list that tells the team exactly what goes in each box.
3. The team assembles the kits in advance of the ship date.
4. When Shopify sends the subscription orders, the pre-built kits ship out immediately.
5. If the kit changes next month, you send updated instructions and any new products.

Kitting also works for product bundles, gift sets, and promotional packages. If you sell a "starter kit" that includes several items, DeliveryGroup can pre-build those kits and ship them as single units. This is faster and more accurate than picking individual items for each order.

## Returns Management

Returns are part of online selling. Customers change their minds, items do not fit, or sometimes things arrive damaged. How you handle returns affects your reputation and your bottom line.

DeliveryGroup processes returns for Shopify sellers. When a customer sends a product back, the warehouse team receives it, inspects it, and updates your inventory. If the item is in good condition, it goes back on the shelf and is ready to sell again. If it is damaged, the team sets it aside and notifies you.

Here is why having your 3PL handle returns matters:

- **Speed.** Returns get processed quickly, which means faster refunds for your customers. Happy customers are more likely to buy again.
- **Accuracy.** The warehouse team inspects every return and updates inventory in real time. You always know exactly what you have in stock.
- **Convenience.** You do not have to deal with return shipments at your home or office. Everything goes to the warehouse.
- **Data.** Return data helps you understand why customers send things back. You can use that information to improve your products or listings.

A smooth return process builds trust. And trust turns one-time buyers into repeat customers.
