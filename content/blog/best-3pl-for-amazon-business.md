## What Business Buyers Expect

Business buyers are not browsing Amazon for fun. They are spending their company's money. They have bosses to answer to. They have procurement policies to follow. Their expectations reflect that reality.

**Accurate orders.** A consumer who gets the wrong item might return it and move on. A business buyer who gets the wrong item has a project delayed. A hospital that gets the wrong supplies has a real problem. Accuracy is not optional. It is the baseline.

**On-time delivery.** Business buyers often order supplies for specific projects or deadlines. Late delivery can cascade into bigger problems. If your product does not arrive when expected, the buyer finds another supplier.

**Professional packaging.** Business shipments need to arrive in good condition. We are not talking about pretty unboxing experiences. We are talking about solid cartons, proper void fill, and packaging that survives a loading dock. A pallet of product that arrives damaged creates a headache for everyone.

**Purchase order support.** Many business buyers issue purchase orders with specific PO numbers. They need that PO number on the invoice and packing slip. Their accounts payable department matches the PO to the invoice for payment. If the numbers do not match, your payment gets delayed.

**Quantity consistency.** When a business buyer orders 100 units, they expect 100 units. Not 98. Not 102. Exactly 100. Short-ships create problems on their end. Over-ships create confusion.

DeliveryGroup meets all of these expectations. Our fulfillment process is built for the precision that business buyers demand.

## Purchase Order Tracking and Management

Purchase orders are the backbone of B2B commerce. When a business buyer issues a PO, it creates a paper trail that flows through their entire organization. Procurement creates it. The receiving dock matches it. Accounts payable pays against it.

Your fulfillment operation needs to support this workflow. That means tracking PO numbers from order receipt through shipment and delivery. It means including PO references on all documentation. It means being able to look up any order by PO number at any time.

DeliveryGroup integrates PO tracking into our standard workflow for Amazon Business sellers. Here is how it works.

If the business buyer contacts you about a specific PO, you can look it up instantly. You see when it was received, when it was picked, when it shipped, and the tracking number. No digging through spreadsheets. No guessing.

## Bulk Packaging That Survives the Journey

Consumer shipments go to front porches. Business shipments go to loading docks. The difference matters.

Loading docks are rough environments. Packages get stacked on pallets, moved by forklifts, and stored in warehouses before someone opens them. Your packaging needs to survive all of that without damage to the product inside.

DeliveryGroup uses packaging methods designed for B2B shipping. Here is what that looks like.

**Right-sized cartons.** We select the appropriate box size for each order. Too big and the product moves around and gets damaged. Too small and the carton bursts. We match the box to the contents.

**Proper void fill.** Empty space inside a carton is where damage happens. We fill voids with appropriate materials. Air pillows, paper, or foam depending on the product and the customer's requirements.

**Reinforced sealing.** B2B cartons get sealed with proper tape on all seams. We do not skimp on this step. A carton that opens in transit is a damaged shipment.

**Case packing for large orders.** When a business buyer orders in quantity, we pack products into standard case quantities inside shipping cartons. This makes receiving easier on their end and protects the products in transit.

**Palletizing for freight shipments.** Large orders get palletized with proper stacking, stretch wrap, and labeling. We build stable pallets that do not shift in transit.

For sellers with specific packaging requirements, we customize the process. Some products need special handling. Some buyers have packaging standards. We document and follow those requirements for every order.
