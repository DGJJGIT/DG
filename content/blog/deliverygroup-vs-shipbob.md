Choosing a 3PL is one of the biggest decisions an e-commerce brand can make. The right partner helps you ship faster, save money, and grow without headaches. The wrong one costs you customers and eats into your margins.

Two names that come up often are DeliveryGroup and ShipBob. Both are established fulfillment providers, but they take very different approaches. This article breaks down the key differences so you can decide which one fits your business.

## Company Overview

**ShipBob** is a large, venture-backed 3PL with over 50 fulfillment centers across the US and internationally. They focus on distributed fulfillment, meaning your inventory is spread across multiple warehouses to reduce shipping distance to customers.

**DeliveryGroup** operates from a strategic 75,000 square foot facility in Northern Kentucky, just 5 miles from both Amazon's CVG Air Hub and DHL's CVG Super Hub. Rather than spreading inventory thin across dozens of locations, DeliveryGroup uses its central location and direct DHL partnership to reach over 80 percent of the US population within 2 to 3 business days by ground.

## Shipping Speed and Cost

This is where the two models differ most.

ShipBob's distributed model means some orders ship from nearby warehouses, but it also means your inventory is split across locations. If a customer orders an item that is not stocked at the nearest warehouse, it ships from further away. You also need enough inventory at each location to avoid stockouts.

DeliveryGroup takes a different approach. By operating from one of the best shipping locations in the country and injecting packages directly into DHL's global hub, every order gets the same fast, affordable shipping. There is no inventory splitting, no stockout risk at satellite locations, and no confusion about where your products are.

DHL's CVG Super Hub is one of only three in the world. DeliveryGroup's direct-inject partnership means packages enter the DHL network at the earliest possible point, resulting in faster transit times and lower shipping rates than most 3PLs can offer.

## FBA Prep Services

This is a major differentiator. **DeliveryGroup offers full Amazon FBA prep services.** That includes FNSKU labeling, poly bagging, bundling, kitting, shrink wrapping, inspection, and shipping plan creation. Their facility is just 5 miles from Amazon's CVG Air Hub, which means inventory reaches Amazon's shelves the same day it leaves the dock.

**ShipBob does not offer traditional FBA prep.** If you sell on Amazon through FBA, you would need a separate prep center in addition to ShipBob. With DeliveryGroup, you can run FBA prep and merchant fulfilled orders from the same inventory pool under one roof.

## Technology and Integrations

Both platforms integrate with major e-commerce platforms like Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, and Amazon. ShipBob has a well-known dashboard with inventory analytics and demand forecasting features.

DeliveryGroup integrates with over 200 platforms and offers a REST API for custom integrations. Their real-time dashboard provides shipment visibility, GPS tracking, delivery status updates, and performance reporting. For enterprise clients, they also support EDI integrations for retail compliance.

## Pricing Model

ShipBob's pricing includes pick and pack fees, storage fees, and shipping costs. Their distributed model can mean higher storage costs because you are storing inventory in multiple locations. There can also be additional fees for special projects and receiving.

DeliveryGroup offers transparent, all-inclusive per-shipment pricing with no hidden fees. Storage is a flat $25 per pallet per month. Because everything ships from one location, there are no duplicate storage charges or inventory allocation complexities.

## Customer Service

ShipBob assigns support through a ticketing system. As a large operation handling thousands of brands, individual attention can vary.

DeliveryGroup assigns a dedicated account manager to every client from day one. You have a single point of contact who knows your business, your products, and your requirements. That level of personal service is a meaningful difference for brands that value a true partnership over a transactional relationship.

## Who Is Each Best For?

**ShipBob is a good fit if:**
- You need fulfillment centers in multiple countries
- You want distributed inventory across many US locations
- You do not sell on Amazon FBA
- You are comfortable with a more self-service model

**DeliveryGroup is a good fit if:**
- You sell on Amazon and need FBA prep under the same roof as 3PL fulfillment
- You want the lowest possible shipping rates through DHL's direct-inject partnership
- You value a dedicated account manager and personalized service
- You want one inventory pool, not split across multiple locations
- You ship supplements, apparel, or products requiring lot tracking

## The Bottom Line

Both are capable 3PLs, but they serve different needs. If your priority is distributed multi-location fulfillment with a global footprint, ShipBob offers that at scale. If your priority is shipping cost savings, FBA prep under one roof, dedicated service, and a strategically located central hub, DeliveryGroup delivers more value for most e-commerce brands.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I use DeliveryGroup for FBA prep and ShipBob for DTC fulfillment?**
You could, but managing two fulfillment partners means splitting inventory and doubling your operational complexity. DeliveryGroup handles both FBA prep and DTC fulfillment from one location, which simplifies everything.

**Is ShipBob cheaper than DeliveryGroup?**
It depends on your volume and shipping zones. ShipBob's distributed model can reduce shipping costs for some zones but increases storage costs. DeliveryGroup's DHL partnership often results in lower total shipping costs, especially for brands shipping nationwide.

**Does DeliveryGroup have multiple warehouse locations?**
DeliveryGroup operates from a single strategic facility in Northern Kentucky. This location is in the geographic center of US population density, within 2-3 day ground reach of over 80 percent of the country.

**Which 3PL is better for Shopify sellers?**
Both integrate natively with Shopify. DeliveryGroup is the better choice if you also sell on Amazon, need FBA prep, or want discounted DHL rates.

**Do both 3PLs handle returns?**
Yes. Both offer returns processing. DeliveryGroup provides consumer-facing returns portals, return label generation, and returned goods inspection and restocking.

**How fast can I switch from ShipBob to DeliveryGroup?**
Most transitions take 2 to 4 weeks. DeliveryGroup assigns a dedicated implementation specialist to manage the migration, including platform integration, inventory transfer, and go-live support.
