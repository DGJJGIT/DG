## Why Apparel Fulfillment Is Different

Shipping clothing is not the same as shipping hard goods. A t-shirt needs to be folded neatly. A dress needs to avoid wrinkles. A delicate blouse needs tissue paper to prevent damage. Shoes need to be paired and boxed properly.

Most general 3PLs treat all products the same. They toss your items in a box and slap a label on it. That works fine for a water bottle or a tool set. It does not work for a $75 vintage jacket that your buyer is excited to receive.

Your Poshmark reputation depends on presentation. Buyers leave detailed reviews. They take photos of how items arrive. A wrinkled, balled-up garment in a plain bag gets a bad review. A neatly folded item wrapped in tissue paper gets a five-star rating and a repeat customer.

DeliveryGroup understands this. We handle every piece of clothing with the care it deserves.

## Poly Bagging for Protection

Poly bags are essential for shipping clothing. They protect garments from moisture, dirt, and damage during transit. A poly bag keeps a white blouse clean even if the outer package gets wet in the rain.

DeliveryGroup offers poly bagging as part of our fulfillment service. When we pack a Poshmark order, we place each garment in a clear poly bag before putting it in the shipping package. This adds a layer of protection that buyers notice and appreciate.

Poly bags also make items look more professional. When a buyer opens their package and sees a clean, sealed poly bag, they feel like they are getting something special. It elevates the unboxing experience without adding much cost.

For sellers who ship bundles or multiple items, we bag each piece individually. This keeps items separated and prevents color transfer or friction damage between garments.

## Tissue Wrapping for a Premium Touch

Some Poshmark sellers want to go beyond basic packaging. They want their items to feel like a gift. Tissue paper makes that happen.

DeliveryGroup offers tissue wrapping for sellers who want a premium unboxing experience. We wrap each garment in tissue paper before placing it in the poly bag or shipping package. This creates a layered presentation that feels special.

Tissue wrapping is especially popular for higher-priced items. If you sell designer clothing or luxury resale, tissue paper matches the quality of your products. Buyers who spend $100 or more on a dress expect a certain level of care. Tissue wrapping delivers that.

You choose how much or how little packaging you want. Basic poly bag for everyday items. Tissue wrap plus poly bag for premium pieces. We customize our process to match your brand.

## Hanger Storage for Delicate Items

Some clothing should not be folded and stored in bins. Blazers lose their shape. Formal dresses get wrinkled. Leather jackets develop creases. These items need to hang.

DeliveryGroup offers hanger storage for apparel inventory that needs it. We hang garments on racks in our warehouse. They stay in good condition and are ready to ship at any time.

This is a big deal for Poshmark sellers who deal in higher-end clothing. If you sell blazers, coats, evening gowns, or structured garments, hanger storage protects your investment. The item arrives at the buyer looking just as good as it did in your listing photos.

Not every garment needs hanger storage. T-shirts, jeans, and casual wear store fine in bins. We work with you to figure out which items should hang and which can be folded. This keeps your storage costs reasonable while protecting the items that need it most.

## Multi-Channel Selling: Poshmark, Mercari, and eBay

Smart resellers do not limit themselves to one platform. If you sell on Poshmark, you should probably also list on Mercari and eBay. Each platform has a different audience. Listing on all three gives you more exposure and more sales.

But selling on multiple platforms creates a headache. If you sell a jacket on Poshmark, you need to immediately remove it from Mercari and eBay. Otherwise, you might sell it twice and have to cancel an order. That hurts your seller rating on both platforms.

DeliveryGroup handles multi-channel fulfillment from one warehouse. All your inventory lives in one place. When an item sells on any platform, it gets pulled from stock. You update your listings, and we handle the shipping.

This makes cross-listing simple and safe. You do not have to worry about overselling. You do not have to maintain separate inventory counts for each platform. One warehouse, one inventory, multiple sales channels.

## Handling Returns With Care

Returns are part of resale. Buyers change their minds. Items do not fit. Sometimes a listing description was not quite right. Whatever the reason, returns need to be handled quickly and professionally.

DeliveryGroup processes returns for Poshmark sellers. When a buyer returns an item, it comes back to our warehouse. We inspect it to make sure it is in the same condition it was shipped. If everything checks out, we put it back in your inventory and it is ready to sell again.

This saves you from having returns showing up at your doorstep. Everything stays in the warehouse. Your inventory stays organized. And you can relist the item as soon as we confirm it is in good shape.

## Quality Control Before Shipping

Poshmark buyers rely on accurate listings. They want the item they see in the photos. DeliveryGroup helps ensure quality control before items go out the door.

When we pack an order, our team checks the item against the listing details. Correct size. Correct color. Correct condition. If something does not match, we flag it before it ships. This catches problems before they become returns.

For resale sellers, this extra check is valuable. When you have hundreds of similar items in storage, mistakes can happen. A size medium shirt looks a lot like a size large. Our barcode system and quality checks prevent those errors.
