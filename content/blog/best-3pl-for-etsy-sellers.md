Etsy separates "production time" from "shipping time" in every listing's estimated delivery window, and tracks your on-time shipping rate as one of the core metrics behind Star Seller status. That distinction matters for fulfillment specifically: once an item is ready to ship, Etsy is measuring how consistently you hit the shipping portion of that promise — which is exactly the part a 3PL takes over, separate from whatever production or make-time your listing states.

## Star Seller Status Runs on Shipping-Time Metrics You Can Actually Control

Etsy's Star Seller badge is awarded based on message response rate, review score, and on-time shipping/tracking rate over a rolling period. Once an item is off the production side and ready to ship, hitting Etsy's stated shipping estimate consistently is squarely a fulfillment-operations question. DeliveryGroup's same-day pick-and-pack for orders received before the daily cutoff keeps the shipping side of that metric solid, so a slow warehouse day doesn't erode a Star Seller badge you've built through good production and customer service.

## Personalization Text Fields Need to Travel With the Order

Etsy listings frequently include personalization fields — custom engraving text, a monogram, a name for a name tag — collected at checkout and attached to the order. A generic fulfillment integration that only reads standard line-item data will drop this information entirely, since it isn't part of a normal SKU/quantity order structure. DeliveryGroup's Etsy integration reads personalization data through Etsy's Open API v3 and carries it through to the warehouse team, so a monogram card or a pre-defined engraving selection actually makes it onto the item that ships — light personalization within pre-built options, not full custom manufacturing, which remains the seller's own production step for true handmade or made-to-order goods.

## Etsy's Own Shipping Labels vs. Warehouse-Generated Labels

Etsy generates discounted shipping labels in-app (through its own USPS and UPS integrations), built around an individual seller printing a label per sale. At warehouse volume, DeliveryGroup's integration pulls the label and rate tied to each Etsy sale and applies it during packing — same label, same rate, executed at pick-pack speed instead of one at a time.

## Branded Packaging for a Craft-and-Story-Driven Marketplace

Etsy buyers are shopping for a story and a maker's brand as much as a product, which makes the physical unboxing experience matter more here than on a pure price-comparison marketplace. DeliveryGroup supports custom packaging, branded tissue paper, and printed inserts — a handwritten-style thank-you card, a care instruction card, a discount code for a repeat purchase — applied consistently to every order once the materials and instructions are on file, so a warehouse-fulfilled order still reads as personally packed.

## Variations and Multi-Quantity Listings

Etsy variations (size, color, material) map to individual SKUs the same way they do on other platforms, and DeliveryGroup's inventory sync tracks each variation separately so a specific color or size selling out doesn't silently block sales of others under the same listing.
