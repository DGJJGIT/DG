## Why Etsy Sellers Hesitate to Use a 3PL

Most Etsy sellers start their business with their own two hands. You make the products, photograph them, write the listings, pack every order, and drive to the post office. You know exactly what goes in every box because you put it there yourself.

Handing that off to someone else feels risky. Here are the most common concerns we hear:

- **"My packaging is part of my brand."** Etsy buyers expect beautiful, thoughtful packaging. A generic brown box with a packing slip does not cut it.
- **"I include personal touches."** Thank-you cards, stickers, free samples, discount codes. These things build customer loyalty and drive repeat purchases.
- **"My products are fragile or unique."** Handmade items, custom orders, and delicate products need special care during packing and shipping.
- **"I do not ship enough to justify a warehouse."** Many Etsy sellers think 3PLs are only for companies shipping thousands of orders a day.
- **"I am afraid of losing control."** When someone else is packing your orders, you worry about quality and consistency.

These are all valid concerns. And they are exactly why most generic 3PLs are a bad fit for Etsy sellers. But DeliveryGroup is not a generic 3PL. We specialize in working with brands that care about the details.

## Handling Growth From 100 Orders a Week and Beyond

There is a tipping point in every Etsy business. When you are doing 20 or 30 orders a week, packing them yourself is manageable. It is time-consuming, but doable. But when you cross 100 orders a week, fulfillment starts consuming your entire schedule.

At 100 orders a week, you are spending several hours every day just packing and shipping. That is time you could spend designing new products, improving your listings, running marketing campaigns, or simply resting so you do not burn out.

Here is what the math looks like:

- **At 100 orders per week:** You are packing roughly 15 orders a day. That is about two to three hours of work if each order takes 10 to 15 minutes.
- **At 200 orders per week:** You are packing 30 orders a day. That is a full-time job just for fulfillment.
- **At 500 orders per week:** You either hire staff and rent warehouse space, or you partner with a 3PL. There is no other way.

DeliveryGroup helps Etsy sellers make the jump from doing it all themselves to having a professional team handle fulfillment. You keep creative control over your brand, your packaging, and your customer experience. We just handle the physical work of getting boxes out the door.

Many of our Etsy clients tell us that partnering with us was the best business decision they made. Not because we are special, but because getting fulfillment off their plate freed them up to focus on what they are actually good at: creating amazing products.
